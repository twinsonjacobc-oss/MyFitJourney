Aim for roughly 2.5–3 L water as a starting guide, adjusting for Qatar's heat, sweating and your clinician's advice. Prioritise 7–9 hours of sleep.

Kerala-style meal plan

Monday3 meals

Portion guide

  • Rice: about 1 level cup cooked per meal.
  • Chapati: 2 medium pieces.
  • Chicken: about 150 g cooked edible portion.
  • Vegetables: 1–2 cups with lunch and dinner.
  • Kadala: ¾–1 cup; use measured oil and moderate coconut.
  • Fruit: 1 medium portion.

Food preferences included

Chicken, eggs, vegetables, fruit, rice and chapati. No fish, mutton or milk. Use water to prepare oats; consider calcium-rich alternatives such as leafy greens, sesame, calcium-set tofu or a suitable fortified plant drink.

Home workout plan

Monday30 minutes

5 min warm-up · 20 min main session · 5 min cool-down. Start with 2 comfortable rounds; rest 45–60 seconds as needed.

Safety first

Use a sturdy chair and a clear floor. Keep movements controlled. Stop if you experience chest pain, faintness, unusual breathlessness or sharp pain. Build up gradually.
